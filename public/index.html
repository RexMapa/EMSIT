QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Built on
clear expectations.

How is the monthly price set?+

Pricing depends on the role, seniority and experience required, working hours and agreed scope. The figures shown are illustrative, from EMSIT’s published comparison. We confirm the final price for your role before you contract.

What’s included in the arrangement?+

Recruitment, employment administration, workplace setup, attendance visibility, security arrangements, and ongoing support are scoped and confirmed before contracting. Specific equipment, IT, and replacement arrangements depend on your package.

How do we handle security and access?+

Before sensitive systems are accessed, we agree on work location, device ownership, named accounts and permissions, confidentiality, data handling, and incident escalation. Controls are tailored with your team.

How quickly can we get started?+

Recruiting timing is estimated after we calibrate the role and salary. Once you select a candidate, we coordinate setup and onboarding while your team leads job-specific training.

Is this outsourcing or a dedicated hire?+

This is managed outstaffing: a dedicated employee works within your workflows under your direction. EMSIT supports the agreed people infrastructure. Your business retains responsibility for priorities, review, and approval.
